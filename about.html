About Walchand College of Engineering

Building Excellence in Engineering Education Since 1947

Our Legacy of Excellence

Founded in 1947, Walchand College of Engineering, Sangli (WCE) is one of India’s oldest and most prestigious private engineering institutions. Spread across a scenic 90-acre campus, WCE offers a unique blend of undergraduate, postgraduate, doctoral, and diploma programs under one roof. With autonomous status, AICTE approval, and an ‘A’ grade from NAAC, the institute has been a pioneer in quality engineering education, fostering innovation, research, and excellence for over 75 years.

At WCE, students are empowered to become leaders and innovators. With a vibrant campus life, active student clubs, and professional chapters, the institute encourages holistic development beyond academics. Graduates of WCE have gone on to excel in top companies worldwide, including Google, Microsoft, Amazon, TCS, and Infosys, reflecting the institute’s enduring legacy of preparing students for impactful careers and a rapidly evolving technological world.

With over 10,000 successful alumni working in leading companies worldwide, we continue to uphold our tradition of excellence while adapting to the changing needs of technology and industry.
