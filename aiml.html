Department Overview

The AI & ML department is at the forefront of technological innovation, established in 2018 to meet the growing demand for AI specialists. Our program combines theoretical foundations with practical applications in machine learning, deep learning, and artificial intelligence.

Students work on cutting-edge research in Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Robotics, and Neural Networks. Our curriculum is designed in collaboration with leading AI research labs and tech companies.

With access to GPU clusters and cloud computing resources, students gain hands-on experience building and deploying AI solutions for real-world problems.

Academic Framework & Outcomes

Vision

To develop competent AI & ML professionals equipped with strong technical knowledge, innovation capability and ethical values to solve real-world engineering problems.

Mission

  • • Deliver quality education in AI & ML
  • • Promote research and innovation
  • • Strengthen industry collaboration
  • • Build ethical intelligent systems
