Department Overview

The Electrical Engineering department has been a cornerstone of WCE since its inception, providing strong foundations in electrical systems, power engineering, and electronics. Our program blends rigorous theoretical learning with hands-on laboratory experience to prepare students for diverse engineering challenges.

Students engage in research and projects in areas such as Power Systems, Renewable Energy, Smart Grids, Control Systems, and Embedded Electronics. The curriculum is designed in collaboration with leading industry partners and research organizations to ensure alignment with the latest technological advancements.

With access to advanced labs, simulation tools, and industry-standard equipment, students gain practical experience designing, analyzing, and implementing electrical solutions for real-world applications, equipping them for successful careers in power, automation, and electronics engineering.

Curriculum

First & Second Year

  • Engineering Mathematics I & II
  • Basic Electrical & Electronics Engineering
  • Programming in C & Python
  • Physics & Chemistry for Engineers
  • Electrical Circuits & Networks
  • Digital Electronics & Logic Design
  • Signals & Systems
  • Engineering Drawing & Workshop Practices

Third & Fourth Year

  • Power Systems Analysis
  • Electrical Machines I & II
  • Control Systems
  • Power Electronics & Drives
  • Renewable Energy Systems
  • High Voltage Engineering
  • Microelectronics & Embedded Systems
  • Capstone Project / Industrial Training
