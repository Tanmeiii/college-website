Department Overview

The Computer Science Engineering department is the flagship program of Walchand College of Engineering, established in 1986. With a legacy of producing exceptional software engineers, we've consistently maintained a 98% placement record with students placed in top global companies.

Our curriculum is designed in collaboration with industry leaders to ensure students are equipped with the latest technologies and industry practices. We focus on practical, hands-on learning through projects, internships, and real-world problem-solving.

Students learn everything from fundamental programming to advanced topics like cloud computing, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and full-stack development.
