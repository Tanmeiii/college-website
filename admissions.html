Eligibility Criteria

Academic Requirements

  • Passed 12th/Intermediate with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics
  • Minimum 60% aggregate marks (55% for reserved categories)
  • Valid JEE Main score required
  • Age limit: Should not exceed 25 years as on July 1, 2026

Required Documents

  • 10th & 12th Marksheets and Certificates
  • JEE Main Scorecard
  • Transfer Certificate from previous institution
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport size photographs (4 copies)
  • Aadhar Card & other ID proofs
